# 08 – Theatre Owner Management

## 1. Exhibitor Partner Governance
Super Admin oversees all registered theatre partner organizations:
- Registered business entities, owner names, contact numbers, and registered addresses.
- Associated cinema complexes and branches per owner.
- Approval status tracking (`PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `SUSPENDED`).
- Verification of tax identifiers (GSTIN, PAN) submitted during onboarding.
