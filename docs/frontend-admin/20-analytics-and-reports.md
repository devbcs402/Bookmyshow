# 20 – Analytics and Reports

## 1. Analytics Subsystems
Powered by `apps/backend/src/modules/analytics/admin-analytics.routes.js`:
- `/admin/analytics/overview`: High-level platform KPIs.
- `/admin/analytics/bookings`: Booking trends and status ratios.
- `/admin/analytics/revenue`: Comprehensive financial breakdown.
- `/admin/analytics/movies`: Top ranked movies by revenue and ticket volume.
- `/admin/analytics/theatres`: Top ranked cinema complexes.
