# 10 – Theatre and Branch Management

## 1. Platform-Wide Multiplex Oversight
Super Admin can inspect every cinema property registered on the platform:
- Master complex attributes: registered company name, address, city, state, postal PIN code, contact details, GSTIN/PAN.
- Operational branches: physical multiplexes, screen counts, guest amenities (IMAX, Dolby Atmos, Parking, Food Court).
- Administrative actions: Update theatre metadata via `PATCH /api/v1/admin/theatres/:id`.
