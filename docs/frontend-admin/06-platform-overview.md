# 06 – Platform Master Overview

## 1. Executive KPIs
Powered by `GET /api/v1/admin/analytics/overview` with dynamic time ranges:
- **Total & Confirmed Bookings**: Overall ticket reservation volume.
- **Gross Ticket Sales & Net Platform Commission**: Total financial throughput and platform commission cut.
- **Active Theatres & Screens**: Deployed exhibitor physical hardware footprint.
- **Pending Approvals Alert**: Real-time counter of theatres and movies awaiting Super Admin verification.

---

## 2. Platform Telemetry Charts
- **Daily Booking & Revenue Trends**: Visual comparison of confirmed ticket admissions and sales volume over time.
- **Booking Status Ratios**: Donut visualization breaking down `CONFIRMED`, `CANCELLED`, and `EXPIRED` transactions.
- **Top Performing Movies & Theatres**: Ranked leaderboards identifying high-grossing films and cinema complexes.
