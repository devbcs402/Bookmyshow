# 19 – Platform Notifications and Broadcast Announcements

## 1. Omnichannel Announcement Engine
Mounted under `/api/v1/admin/notifications`:
- `POST /broadcast`: Broadcasts platform announcements to target user segments across Email, SMS, WhatsApp, and In-App channels.
- `GET /stats`: Delivery metrics, channel success rates, and queue health.
- `GET /`: Platform-wide notification audit trail.
